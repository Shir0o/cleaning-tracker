# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming.

## Expected Behavior
- Use clear and respectful language.
- Assume good intent while staying precise about technical concerns.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them when possible.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or repeated disruptive comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing secrets, credentials, or private user data.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants whose behavior is harmful to the project or its contributors.
